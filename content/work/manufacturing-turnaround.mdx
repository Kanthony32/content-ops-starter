## Situation

A 75-year-old manufacturing company faced an existential crisis:

- Five consecutive years of declining revenue
- Operating margins compressed from 12% to 3%
- Quality issues driving customer attrition
- Outdated equipment and processes
- Low employee morale and high turnover

The board brought in new leadership and engaged us to lead the operational transformation.

## Diagnostic Phase

We conducted a rapid 30-day assessment:

### Key Findings

**Production Inefficiencies**
- Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE) at 45% (vs. 85% industry benchmark)
- Setup times 3-4x longer than best-in-class
- High scrap rates and rework

**Quality Issues**
- Defect rate of 8% (industry average: 2%)
- Customer complaints up 150% over 3 years
- Reactive rather than preventive quality management

**Supply Chain Gaps**
- Inventory turns at 4x (industry: 12x)
- Supplier quality issues causing production delays
- No formal supplier development program

## Transformation Program

We designed a comprehensive 18-month program:

### Wave 1: Stabilize (Months 1-6)

**Immediate Actions**
- Implemented daily management system for shop floor
- Created rapid response team for quality issues
- Launched Kaizen events for quick improvements

**Results**
- OEE improved from 45% to 62%
- Defect rate reduced from 8% to 5%
- On-time delivery improved from 72% to 88%

### Wave 2: Optimize (Months 7-12)

**Systematic Improvements**
- Rolled out Total Productive Maintenance (TPM)
- Implemented statistical process control
- Redesigned plant layout for flow optimization
- Launched supplier development program

**Results**
- OEE reached 75%
- Defect rate down to 2.5%
- Lead times reduced by 40%

### Wave 3: Sustain (Months 13-18)

**Capability Building**
- Trained 200+ employees in Lean/Six Sigma
- Established continuous improvement culture
- Implemented performance management system
- Created innovation program for employee ideas

**Results**
- OEE sustained above 80%
- Defect rate below 1.5%
- 500+ improvement ideas implemented

## Business Impact

### Financial Performance

- **Revenue growth**: +35% over 18 months
- **Operating margin**: 3% → 11%
- **EBITDA**: +$45M improvement
- **Working capital**: Freed up $18M

### Operational Metrics

- **OEE**: 45% → 82% (+82%)
- **Quality**: Defects from 8% to 1.2%
- **On-time delivery**: 72% → 96%
- **Inventory turns**: 4x → 10x

### People & Culture

- **Employee turnover**: Reduced from 28% to 12%
- **Safety**: Zero lost-time accidents for 12+ months
- **Engagement**: Employee satisfaction +45 points
- **Certifications**: 60% of workforce certified in Lean/Six Sigma

## Sustainability Measures

To ensure lasting impact:

1. **Governance**: Monthly leadership reviews of key metrics
2. **Capability**: Ongoing training and development programs
3. **Recognition**: Rewards and incentives tied to continuous improvement
4. **Culture**: "Voice of Employee" program for ongoing feedback

## Two Years Later

The transformation has proven sustainable:

- Operating margins maintained above 10%
- Named "Supplier of the Year" by two major customers
- Expanded into three new product lines
- Won $50M+ in new business
- Industry benchmark for operational excellence
