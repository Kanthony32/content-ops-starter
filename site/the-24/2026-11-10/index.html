The Game · Phoenix

Wemby Needed 25 Minutes. Phoenix Needed a New Game Plan.

Forty points, 11 boards and 9 blocks on 19 shots, and he sat the fourth quarter. San Antonio has won nine straight, and the rim belongs to one man.

By Westview Staff

40 PTS11 REB9 BLK3 AST15-19 FG2-3 3PT8-10 FT12-13 PAINT25 MIN

Victor Wembanyama played 25 minutes in Phoenix on Monday. That was enough for 40 points, 11 rebounds and 9 blocks in a 122-96 Spurs win. He had 22 by halftime and was done before the fourth quarter started, per Yahoo Sports.

Put that in context. Per The Athletic's Jared Weiss, as cited by Yahoo, only nine other players have ever gone for 40, 10 and 8 in a game. The fastest of them needed 38 minutes. Wemby got there 13 minutes sooner. A week ago he hung 52 and 24 on Dallas.

The scouting read: he went 12-of-13 in the paint and 15-of-19 overall. Phoenix had no answer for his catches around the rim, and when the Suns tried to get downhill, he was waiting there. San Antonio blocked 11 shots as a team.

The Spurs are 9-2 after an 0-2 start. Dylan Harper added 17 on 7-of-10 and De'Aaron Fox put up 15, 9 and 6. Devin Booker scored 32 on 10-of-17 for Phoenix, now 3-6.

Scout's Note · The midrange tax

Wemby's real number is what he took away. Phoenix went 26-of-63 on twos (41.3%) and 2-of-18 from midrange. When the rim is closed, you settle for pull-ups, and that's the worst shot in the game. Meanwhile San Antonio scored 62 in the paint and 23 on the break. That's a defensive scheme that writes the other team's shot chart for them.

NBA · what moved last night

Dallas

Flagg Drops 43 and Ties LeBron at 19

Mavericks 125, Pacers 122

Cooper Flagg went for 43 and 10 on 16-of-25 shooting, 8-of-8 at the line. That's a season high and the seventh 35-point game of his career, tying LeBron James for the most by a teenager. Kyrie Irving matched his season best with 35 and seven threes. The two scored Dallas' first 13 points of the fourth quarter.

Indiana led 66-56 at the half behind Pascal Siakam's 26 on 8-of-9 shooting. He scored 3 after the break. Dallas is 7-4. The Pacers are 3-8.

San Francisco

0-3 to Eight Straight. Nobody's Done That Before.

Warriors 138, Pelicans 87

Per Yahoo Sports, Golden State is the first team in NBA history to start 0-3 and then win eight in a row. The 51-point win finished off a 6-0 homestand. Stephen Curry scored 25 in about 20 minutes and went 9-of-9 at the line. Brandin Podziemski added 25, and rookie Yaxel Lendeborg had 23 on 8-of-10, 4-of-4 from deep.

The Warriors assisted on 37 of 50 field goals. New Orleans shot 36.3% and is 4-7.

Atlanta

Luka Runs It Back. The Lakers Sit at 10-2.

Lakers 124, Hawks 114

Luka Doncic went for 32 points, 10 assists, 8 rebounds, 5 steals and 2 blocks, with no Austin Reaves next to him. Sandro Mamukelashvili gave the Lakers 23 off the bench on 5-of-8 from three, and Walker Kessler added 21 and 10.

Los Angeles had 16 steals, turned them into 29 points off turnovers, and has won six straight. They're first in the West. Atlanta falls to 7-5.
