The Game · Toronto

Thirteen Straight Is Over. Herro and Turner Get Milwaukee Its Second Win.

Two nights after dropping 50 in a loss, Tyler Herro scored 32 on 16 shots. Myles Turner popped for five threes. The Bucks are 2-16, and they finally have something to show for it.

By Westview Staff

HERRO 32 PTS10-16 FG4-5 3PT8-8 FT6 REB8 AST15 PTS IN Q3

Milwaukee hadn't won since Oct. 26. Monday in Toronto it ended a 13-game losing streak, the second-longest in franchise history, 116-111. It's the Bucks' second win of the season.

Herro was the engine. Three points at the half, then 15 in the third quarter, and 32 by the horn on 10-of-16 from the field, 4-of-5 from deep and 8-of-8 at the line. He drew nine fouls and handed out eight assists. That's an 82.0% true shooting night from a guard carrying a 2-16 team. He scored a career-high 50 in a loss at Philadelphia this weekend. This time the efficiency came with a W.

Turner gave him the release valve. Season-highs of 27 points and five threes, on 9-of-12 shooting and 5-of-6 from deep, with 11 rebounds before he fouled out. Kyle Kuzma added 18. Rookie Brayden Burries put up 14 points, 10 rebounds, four assists and three steals and finished plus-20.

Scottie Barnes did everything he could for Toronto: 31 points, eight assists, four blocks. The Raptors are 4-11 since Kawhi Leonard went down with his left knee and lost for the seventh time in eight games.

Scout's Note · Why it finally flipped

Milwaukee turned it over 17 times and gave up 26 points off turnovers. Normally that's a loss. It won because the shot profile was clean: 14-of-28 from three (50.0%) and 20-of-24 at the line. Turner spacing as the pick-and-pop big pulled the rim protector out, and Herro attacked the open floor downhill. Toronto went 15-of-22 at the stripe (68.2%) in a five-point game. That's the margin.

NBA · what moved last night

Minneapolis

McDaniels Goes for a Career-High 34 on Randle's Return Night

Timberwolves 142, Nets 116

Jaden McDaniels didn't miss from three (4-of-4), went 12-of-16 overall and added 10 rebounds and six assists. That's a career high and his third 30-point regular-season game. Minnesota hung a season-high 142, forced 21 turnovers and turned them into 31 points.

Julius Randle came back to Target Center for the first time since the offseason four-team trade that brought LaMelo Ball to Minnesota. He got a video tribute and led all scorers with 29 on 11-of-16. Brooklyn shot 50% from three and still lost by 26. The Nets have lost five straight.

Philadelphia

Avdija Hits Five Threes and Hands Philly Its Fourth Loss

Trail Blazers 128, 76ers 111

Deni Avdija had 22 by the 6:03 mark of the second quarter and finished with 31 on 12-of-19. Donovan Clingan was the inside force: 15 points on 7-of-8, 18 rebounds, five assists. Portland hit 19 threes and got 54 from its bench to finish its six-game road trip 3-3.

LeBron James scored a season-high 23 and hit his first four threes. Tyrese Maxey added 22, and Jaylen Brown had 20 in his return. With Joel Embiid out sick, the Sixers went 9-of-33 from deep. Five-game win streak over; Philly is still 14-4 and first in the East.

Indianapolis

Shai Drops 38. OKC Drops Six of Eight.

Pacers 125, Thunder 120

Shai Gilgeous-Alexander scored 38 on 13-of-30 with seven assists, and it wasn't enough. OKC pulled within one or two points five times in the last five minutes, and Indiana answered every time. Pascal Siakam hit a three with 45 seconds left, Kelly Oubre Jr. blocked Ajay Mitchell, and Ivica Zubac dunked it shut.

Siakam and Oubre had 20 apiece, and Zubac went for 14 and 15. Indiana snapped a four-game skid with a season-low nine turnovers. OKC is 10-8 and 10th in the West.

The Business

Front offices · trades · the money side of the game

NBA · Minnesota

The Randle Trade, Checked at 13-5

Minnesota moved Julius Randle in an offseason four-team deal that brought LaMelo Ball from Charlotte. Monday, Randle came back and scored 29. Minnesota won by 26 anyway, and it's 13-5, third in the West, with four wins in its last five. Brooklyn is 3-13. Ball had 17 on 6-of-16.

AP via CBS Minnesota Star Tribune

My Take

Grade the trade on the roster, not the box score. Randle can still go for 29 on 16 shots. What Minnesota bought was a second creator who changes how the floor is spaced and frees McDaniels to be a finisher and a cutter instead of a fourth option standing in the corner. Monday McDaniels took 16 shots and made 12.

The front-office lesson: you don't trade a productive player because he's bad. You trade him because the fit caps your ceiling. Eighteen games in, Minnesota's ceiling looks higher.

Reform Desk

The Basement Is Crowded. Fix the Incentive.

Sacramento is 2-15. Milwaukee is 2-16. Utah is 3-16 and Brooklyn is 3-13. Not every one of those teams is trying to lose, but the lottery rewards the ones that are. When four teams are this far down before Thanksgiving, the league has a product problem. Flatten the odds further or tie picks to multi-year records. Make winning the cheapest path back.
