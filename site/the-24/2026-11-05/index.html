The Game · San Antonio

Wemby Goes 52, 24 and 8. The Spurs Needed Every One.

Career highs in points and rebounds, eight blocks, and a one-point win over Dallas. Six straight for San Antonio, and the league just got a look at what a 7-foot-4 problem looks like when he stops settling.

By The Westview Group · Contributor: Keil Moore

52 PTS16-28 FG1 3PM19-23 FT24 REB8 BLK2 STL2 AST

Victor Wembanyama set career highs with 52 points and 24 rebounds Wednesday, added 8 blocks, and the Spurs held off the Mavericks 112-111. He had 29 points, 15 rebounds and 5 blocks by halftime. Per KSAT, nobody had put up 25, 10 and 5 blocks in a single half since at least 1996-97.

The history goes deeper. Only one other player, Bob McAdoo, has posted 50 points, 20 rebounds and 5 blocks in a game since blocks became an official stat in 1973-74, per IBTimes' research. NBA.com framed it as a line the league hadn't seen in 50 years.

Here's the scouting read: one made three. He scored 51 of his 52 inside the arc or at the line. Fifteen twos and 19 free throws. When Wemby plays the floor instead of floating to the arc, there's no matchup on a roster built to handle it. Dallas sent him to the line 23 times because the other choice was a dunk.

It still came down to one possession. Kyrie Irving scored 29 and missed the potential game-winner. Naji Marshall added 22 and PJ Washington 21. Cooper Flagg went for 19, 10 and 5. For San Antonio, Dylan Harper scored 21 and Stephon Castle had 17 and 10.

Scout's Note · The shot diet

Watch the possessions, not the total. 28 field-goal attempts and 23 free-throw attempts is a post player's diet, not a stretch big's. Dallas had to choose: send help and live with the offensive glass (24 boards), or stay home and send him to the line. They picked the line, and he went 19-of-23. Eight blocks on the other end means every Dallas drive had a tax on it. That's how you win by one on a night your guards don't carry you.

Oklahoma City

Shai Hangs 42 on Cleveland. Again.

Thunder 124, Cavaliers 116 · OKC 6-2

Two games, two 40-pieces against the same opponent. Shai Gilgeous-Alexander scored 43 in Cleveland on Monday and came back with 42 and 8 assists at home: 11-of-26 from the floor, 5-of-11 from three, 15-of-19 at the line. That's the tell. When the jumper is average, he still gets to the stripe 19 times.

Ajay Mitchell added 23, and rookie Bennett Stirtz gave OKC 16 on 4-of-7 from deep. Donovan Mitchell scored 27, James Harden 19, and Evan Mobley posted 16 and 10. Cleveland is 4-4.

NCAAM · NCAAW · Washington

The Midterms Leave College Sports' Federal Fix on Life Support

The Protect College Sports Act passed the Senate 77-22 on Sept. 28. After Tuesday's midterms, Yahoo's FTW called it "effectively dead," with little chance of a House vote in the lame-duck session. Sport & State reported that attorney Darren Heitner puts its odds of failure at 80%, and that a future bill could include collective bargaining for athletes.

Meanwhile, the courts are running the sport. Per the AP, 362 men's and 62 women's basketball players have sued over the NCAA's five-year eligibility model, and 171 men and 30 women have won some form of relief. Saint Louis' Robbie Avila testified Wednesday in his own case. Arizona's RJ Godfrey sat out the UCLA game after a Texas judge declined to grant him a restraining order.

Yahoo / FTW AP via The Item Avila hearing

My Take

201 basketball players are on the floor because a judge said so. That isn't a rulebook. It's a map of county courthouses. Whether you're eligible depends on the state your school is in.

Waiting on Congress was always the wrong game plan. The fix sits with the people who run the sport: write one eligibility standard, a clean clock that every program applies the same way, and sit at the table with the athletes to bargain it. Rules the players agreed to hold up in court. Rules handed down to them get tested one TRO at a time.

Reform Desk · WNBA

Record Finals. Now Price It Like One.

The day Golden State paraded down the Embarcadero, the league released the numbers: the Valkyries-Dream Finals averaged 1.88 million viewers, the most since 1999 and up 30%. Game 7 drew 3.3 million, the most-watched WNBA postseason game ever. All seven games sold out, 123,553 total. That's the audience. The next deals, from media to the CBA to the Finals schedule, should be built on these numbers, not on yesterday's assumptions.
