NBA CUP · East Group B

Giannis Is Questionable, Embiid Doubtful, and the 5 PM Tip Hangs on Both

Heat at 76ers tips at 5:00 PM ET on ESPN. Giannis Antetokounmpo (groin) missed Sunday's win over Oklahoma City, took part in most of Tuesday's practice and is questionable. Joel Embiid is listed doubtful with an illness. NBA.com's preview framed East Group B (Cleveland, New York, Miami, Philadelphia, Indiana) as a five-team race for one guaranteed quarterfinal spot, so this one carries weight.

The read: if Giannis plays and Embiid sits, Miami holds the edge in the middle of the floor, and Philadelphia has to win with Jaylen Brown and LeBron James on the perimeter. We will know more closer to tip.

NBA CUP · West

Wolves Without Edwards at OKC. Spurs at Portland Waiting on Wemby and Fox.

Minnesota is 13-5 and first in the league in defensive rating (104.4) per Hoops Rumors, but it visits Oklahoma City (7:30 PM ET, ESPN) without Anthony Edwards (knee, out since Oct. 26), Donte DiVincenzo and Jaylen Clark. The Thunder list Chet Holmgren, Jalen Williams, Cason Wallace and Thomas Sorber out. Alex Caruso, who missed three games with a hip impingement, is available to return.

San Antonio (15-3, 8-0 on the road) is at Portland at 10:00 PM ET on ESPN. Victor Wembanyama (low back tightness) and De'Aaron Fox (right shin contusion) are questionable after both sat out the Spurs' road win at Sacramento. Dylan Harper, Tobias Harris and Jayden Quaintance are out. Portland (11-6) is without Ja Morant (Grade 2 hamstring, out until at least Dec. 1), Shaedon Sharpe and Vit Krejci. The Spurs are 2-0 in West Group C and finish Friday against the Lakers.

NCAAM · SEC

SEC and LSU Head to Court Today. The League Vote Is Dec. 3.

CBS Sports reports a hearing is set for Wednesday in Birmingham, before Judge Annemarie Carney Axon, on the SEC's motion to stop LSU officials from blocking the Dec. 3 presidents' meeting or undoing its outcome in another court. Expulsion would take 11 of 16 presidents and chancellors, and CBS says it is still considered unlikely. CBS also reports RJ Luis Jr. has since left the LSU program.

LSU plays Arizona State at 2:00 PM ET on CBS Sports Network. The filings are allegations until a court rules, and LSU's side is thinly reported so far.

NBA · Wire

Quiet Wire: Kings Add Vincent, Bulls Waive Robinson, Porter Jr. Meets With Orlando

Hoops Rumors' transactions log shows nothing dated Tuesday or Wednesday. The latest moves are Monday's: Sacramento signed guard Gabe Vincent to a non-guaranteed deal for the rest of the season, which lets Malik Monk move back to shooting guard, and Chicago waived Orlando Robinson, who was on a non-guaranteed minimum deal signed Nov. 13.

Kevin Porter Jr. met with the Magic on Monday, per The Stein Line and confirmed by the Orlando Sentinel's Jason Beede. The outcome and his contract status were not reported. Brandon Ingram made his Clippers debut Monday and shot 6-of-18, 0-of-6 from three.

Keil's Take

Four of the names that drive tonight's biggest games, Giannis, Embiid, Wembanyama and Fox, carry questionable or doubtful tags, and East Group B is playing for one guaranteed quarterfinal spot. The league built a tournament where group games count. It should build access to match. Cleveland at New York, a game in that race, is on MSG, DAZN and League Pass, not national TV. If a game decides a bracket spot, every fan should be able to watch it, and every team should know the availability rules well before tip.
