NBA · Election Day

A dark Tuesday, after a 15-game Monday

The NBA left Tuesday, Nov. 3 off the schedule for Election Day. That meant a 15-game Monday with all 30 teams on the floor. The finish came in New Orleans, where the Pelicans beat the Grizzlies 135-134 in overtime. Play resumes Wednesday with a 14-game slate.

Keil's Take

The league traded one night of inventory for a civic day, and nothing broke. A 15-game Monday and a dark Tuesday show the calendar can flex when somebody decides it should. Keep that in mind the next time we are told the back-to-backs, the travel and the midweek college grind are fixed. They are decisions too, and decisions can be revisited.
