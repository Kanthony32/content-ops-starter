NBA · Breaking · Pelicans

Murray Asks for a Trade. Phoenix Is Calling.

Dejounte Murray wants out of New Orleans, and the Pelicans are working with his agency on a resolution, per reports relayed by Hoops Rumors at 2:05 PM. Chris Haynes reports the Suns have interest. The contract is the hard part: about $32.8 million this season, plus a $30.8 million player option for 2027-28. That's real salary to match for any buyer.

He's officially out tonight against Memphis with an ankle injury. The reporting ties the absence to the trade request, so treat him as out until he's moved.

Game to Watch · 9:30 PM · Prime

Warriors at Spurs: Can Anyone Guard the Paint Without Curry's Gravity?

San Antonio is 10-2 and hasn't lost since the first week; Golden State is 8-4 but down Curry, Butler and Moody. Without Curry, there's no off-ball gravity pulling Wembanyama out of the paint. He can sit at the rim as a free safety and wall off drives. The scout's number: Wembanyama had 32, 9 and 5 blocks last time out. If the Warriors can't make him guard in space, through spread pick-and-roll or a five who can shoot, this becomes a jump-shooting contest they're not built to win. Watch whether Fox's hip lets him get downhill. If he sits, the Spurs' half-court creation thins out fast.

Game to Watch · 8:00 PM

Cavaliers at Heat: 5-6 vs. 5-6, and Cleveland Has No Mobley

Both teams are 5-6. Miami has won three of four but sits in the league's bottom 10 in defensive rating. Cleveland loses Mobley, its best switch defender and help-side eraser, so Giannis gets a matchup he'll attack: no long help-side body waiting at the rim. The tell is early: if the Cavs send a second defender at Giannis in the post and the Heat shooters make them pay, it's over by the third quarter. If Cleveland stays home and lives with Giannis twos, its guard advantage can keep it close.

NBA · Transactions · Bulls

Chicago Fills the 15th Spot With Orlando Robinson

The Bulls are signing center Orlando Robinson, 26, to fill their last standard roster spot. He was in camp on a non-guaranteed deal and was waived before the opener, and this contract is non-guaranteed too, per The Athletic's Joel Lorenzi. It also frees Chicago to use its two-way players more. With Caleb Wilson out 4-6 weeks with a broken hand, they needed the bodies. Remember noon's CBA note: anyone signed from Nov. 12 on can't be traded until after the deadline.

Keil's Take

The injury report says Dejounte Murray has an ankle problem. The reporting says he's out until he's traded. Both can be true. But a report the league publishes for transparency, and that bettors and fans read like scripture, shouldn't leave the public guessing which one it is.

The league already has a "not with team" tag. Make it mandatory and specific when a player is sitting for roster reasons. A medical designation should mean a medical reason. Once the injury report turns into a negotiating tool, it stops being information.
