NCAAW · Battle 4 Atlantis

2,648 and Counting: Hidalgo Takes the Record and the Game

Hannah Hidalgo's three about three minutes in put Notre Dame up 10-0 and moved her past Arike Ogunbowale as the program's career scoring leader. No. 13 Notre Dame beat No. 22 West Virginia 78-64 to reach 7-0. Hidalgo had 22 points, 9 steals, 6 rebounds and 4 assists, going 7-of-17 from the floor, and now has 2,648 points. The noon note said to chart ball security: West Virginia committed 24 turnovers, 18 on Irish steals. Notre Dame also won the offensive glass 21-11. Next on the Irish schedule, per AP: No. 12 Vanderbilt next Thursday.

Georgia Closes on an 11-0 Run in Athens

Business · EuroLeague

Permanent Licenses Head Toward a Mid-December Vote

Reuters, citing two people familiar with the talks, reports EuroLeague clubs are expected to vote in Barcelona on a plan to grant permanent franchise licenses to six clubs and grow from 20 to 24 teams. The six: Valencia, Maxima Roma, Hapoel Tel Aviv, PAOK, Dubai and Paris. Four already play in the league. Virtus Bologna, Partizan and Napoli are seeking three-year wild cards. EuroLeague declined to comment. NBA Europe is the competitive backdrop.

Keil's Take

A permanent license buys a club stability. It also closes a door. If the EuroLeague is going to lock in seats, publish the way in before the vote: the sporting standard, the financial standard and a timeline for any club outside the room. Investors need certainty. So do the clubs developing players and building fan bases with something to climb toward.

NBA Cup · Wire

Seven Knockout Spots Are Open Friday. The Check Has Grown.

Dallas has won West Group A and is the first team through. The other seven spots get decided Friday, with quarterfinals Dec. 4-5 and semifinals Dec. 8-9. Per Hoops Rumors, each player on the champion gets $604,985, about 21% more than the $500,000 of 2023. The runner-up share is $241,994, semifinal losers get $120,997 and quarterfinal losers $60,499. A two-way player on the winner gets $302,493.

On the wire, Orlando signed guard Kevin Porter Jr. to a two-year deal, announced Wednesday. He averaged 17.4 points, 7.4 assists and 5.2 rebounds in 38 games last season. The film question is fit: another ball-handler beside Jalen Suggs and Anthony Black.

NCAAW · Battle 4 Atlantis

Hidalgo Is Tied at 2,626. West Virginia Gets First Shot at Keeping Her There.

Hannah Hidalgo's 22 points against Davidson tied Arike Ogunbowale's Notre Dame career scoring record. Hidalgo got to 2,626 in 109 games to Ogunbowale's 149, or 24.1 points a game to 17.6. No. 13 Notre Dame and No. 22 West Virginia, both 6-0, were set for an 11:30 AM ET tip Thursday on ESPN2.

The scout's read: in a 64-52 game, Davidson held the Irish to 39% shooting and two threes. Notre Dame had been averaging 106.6 points, and it committed 16 turnovers. West Virginia forced 17 turnovers against Florida and turned them into 20 points. Ball security is the tendency to chart.

NCAAM · Maui and the Courts

Colorado State Blows a 27-Point Lead, Then Wins It at the Horn

In Maui's seventh-place game, Colorado State led Clemson 55-28 at the half. Clemson opened the second half with 18 straight points and later took the lead. Jojo McIver's left-handed layup at the buzzer won it for the Rams 77-75. It was his only basket. Colorado State shot 69% before the break and 29% after it. Clemson went from 23% to 63%.

In Fort Worth, a Tarrant County judge ruled Houston guard Chendall Weaver eligible for the rest of the season in his challenge to the NCAA's new eligibility rules. He is averaging 7.8 points, 3.6 rebounds and 1.2 steals in five games. The top-ranked Cougars host Oakland on Friday.

Business · NBA Europe

The NBA Lines Up Capital for a Roughly $11 Billion Arena Push

The league says it is assembling investors to mobilize at least €10 billion for new and renovated European arenas and the districts around them. This is not the NBA writing the whole check. Names include Apollo Sports Capital, Ares, Arctos, Blackstone, HPS, J.P. Morgan, Sixth Street and Stonepeak. The NBA-FIBA league is targeted for fall 2027. The EuroLeague is not backing the project, and franchise cities and owners are not finalized.

Keil's Take

Arena capital is a real commitment to a market, but a building is not a basketball ecosystem. Before anyone calls NBA Europe growth, show the rest of the game plan: youth academy courts, a path for the women's game, access for local clubs and a clear answer for how players move between this league and the clubs that developed them. Put those in writing while the capital is still being assembled. Once the leases are signed, the leverage is gone.
