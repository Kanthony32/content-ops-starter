Game to Watch · Lakers at Hawks · 7:00 PM

Daniels on the Pickup Decides It

Two streaks on the line: Atlanta (7-4) has won four straight, the Lakers five. Atlanta leads the league at 31.5 assists a game and plays at the third-fastest pace. The Lakers lead it at 121.9 points and a 122.5 offensive rating, but they're on the second night of a back-to-back with Reaves questionable. The scout's read is Dyson Daniels (2.9 steals a game) on Dončić's live dribble. If Daniels can pressure the pickup, Atlanta runs. If Luka gets to his spots, Walker Kessler (14.4 points, 10.9 rebounds, 69.4% shooting) feasts on a front line missing Veesaar, Gueye and Newell, with Landale questionable.

Game to Watch · Nuggets at Rockets · 8:30 PM

No Adams, No Capela. Here Comes Jokić.

Houston opened 7-1, has lost two straight, and still carries the league's second-best offense and a top-10 defense. It plays tonight without its two veteran centers, Steven Adams and Clint Capela. NBA · Business

Miami Lines Up the 2028 All-Star Weekend

The NBA plans to name Miami the host of 2028 All-Star Weekend, a report the Miami Herald has confirmed. It would be the Heat's first All-Star Game since 1990, the only time Miami has hosted since the franchise's 1988 founding. The league approached the Heat, not the other way around. Phoenix hosts this season's game in February.

Keil's Take

Week 1 polls tell you who scheduled scared. Michigan's men played a national-title rematch in the first week, lost to the team now ranked No. 4 and dropped four spots. Nobody drops in the poll for winning a buy game. If we want November basketball that matters for players, for TV and for the sport, the people with ballots have to stop treating a loss to a top-five team as a red flag. The selection committee moved to NET and quadrants years ago because the schedule is part of the résumé. The AP poll is still grading on wins and losses in November. Grade the résumé, not the record, and coaches will start scheduling like it.

NBA · Front Office · Golden State

The Warriors Are on a Dec. 16 Porziņģis Clock

Per Marc Stein and Jake Fischer, Golden State is still exploring trades for Kristaps Porziņģis' contract after he announced he is stepping away from basketball, and would prefer to move it before Dec. 16. That's the last day the Warriors could take back salary in a deal and re-aggregate it before the Feb. 11 deadline. The contract is partially guaranteed for next season, Porziņģis has reportedly been willing to discuss a buyout, and the team hasn't ruled out attaching draft capital. He is listed out tonight against New Orleans as not with the team.

NCAAM · NCAAW · Eligibility

The NCAA Won in Denver. It's Still Losing the Map.

Friday's 10th Circuit decision in Wisne v. NCAA reversed a July ruling that had given Class of 2022 high school graduates a fifth year. Judge Carolyn McHugh's opinion said the plaintiffs failed to define a single market for all Division I players. Per the AP, the ruling doesn't touch the roughly 200 state-court cases involving up to 850 athletes. That's why Saint Louis' Robbie Avila can win in a St. Louis courtroom and still sit on game night.

Itoudis Wants EuroLeague Clubs to Field Second Teams

Hapoel Tel Aviv coach Dimitris Itoudis told Eurohoops that every EuroLeague club should run a second team in its domestic league, a "Hapoel Tel Aviv 2," so young players get real minutes while the first team focuses on the EuroLeague. He also took on foreign-player rules that differ country to country. Keil's Take

Itoudis is right, and it isn't only a European problem. A 19-year-old who sits on a EuroLeague bench isn't developing. He's waiting. The NBA built a 31-team G League for exactly this. American college basketball is going the other way: 15-man roster caps, transfer churn, eligibility decided county by county. The player who needs reps doesn't get them. Development needs a guaranteed place to play, a clear runway and coaches who are judged on growth, not just on wins this week. Second teams are a structural answer to a structural problem. Every level of the game should be asking where its next player gets his minutes.
