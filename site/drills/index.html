      How we score a drill

      Each drill gets a 1–5 grade on six questions. The grades are weighted and turned into an effectiveness score out of 100. Game transfer carries the most weight because a drill that looks good in an empty gym and disappears in a game isn’t doing its job.

      • Game transfer30%How much it looks and feels like real possessions.
      • Decision load20%How many real reads the player makes, versus scripted reps.
      • Variability15%Different spots, angles, timing and defenders from rep to rep.
      • Competitive pressure15%A live opponent or a score that raises the intensity.
      • Rep density10%Reps per minute while the quality stays high.
      • Evidence10%Backing from coaching curricula like Jr. NBA and USA Basketball, or published research.
