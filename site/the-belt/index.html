How the belt works

  • The defending NBA champion starts the season with the belt. For 2026-27, that's the New York Knicks.
  • The holder defends it in every game that counts: regular season, NBA Cup group and knockout games, the play-in and the playoffs.
  • Preseason, the All-Star Game and the NBA Cup final don't count.
  • Win and you keep it. Lose and the belt changes hands immediately. There are no draws.
  • Season-reset mode: the lineage starts over each year with the new champion.
